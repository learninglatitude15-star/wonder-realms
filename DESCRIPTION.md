Shrink to the size of a bug, raise and ride elemental dragons, and raid pillager outposts on floating sky islands.

# Wonder Realms

**Three adventures in one mod.** Drink a potion and explore your own world at insect size. Win a dragon egg, raise the hatchling, and fly it into battle. Discover islands floating high in the sky, where wild dragons nest and pillagers sail airships. Each adventure stands on its own, and they connect: tiny trips lead to treasure, treasure leads to the sky, and the sky is where the dragons are.

## 🐜 Tiny Worlds

### Getting small

Brew a **Shrinking Draught** from a glass bottle, a brown mushroom and an amethyst shard. Drink it and you shrink to **a tenth of your size for five minutes**. Grass towers over you like a jungle, a single step becomes a cliff, and a spider is a monster the size of a house.

Want to grow back early? A **Growth Draught** (glass bottle, red mushroom and bone meal) returns you to full size at once.

### Life at bug size

- **Climb walls** just by walking into them.
- **Fall without fear.** You weigh almost nothing, so falls do barely any damage.
- **Find dewdrops.** Break grass and ferns while you're tiny and you'll often find a dewdrop, too small to see at normal size. Drinking one gives you a few seconds of Regeneration.

### Carve blocks pixel by pixel

While you're tiny, mining a block doesn't break it. Instead it chips out a single **pixel**, a cube 1/8 of a block wide. Every block holds 512 of them, so you can carve stone, wood, dirt and almost any plain block into statues, arches, lettering, or anything else you can imagine. Carved blocks keep their original look.

- The block's normal drop comes once you've carved away half of it.
- Your tool only wears down a little: one point of durability every 32 pixels.
- Back at full size, mining a carved block breaks it whole.

### Meet the Pixel Miners

Shrink down and you'll start meeting the **Pixel Miners**, tiny folk who live in the grass and the stone. Up to four gather around you on the surface or deep in a cave. They chip away at rocks, wander about, and run from spiders. Give one a **dewdrop** and it pays you from its diggings:

| Payment | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| Iron nuggets | 45% |
| Gold nuggets | 25% |
| Redstone | 15% |
| Diamond Pixels | 12% |
| Emerald Pixels | 3% |

Nine **Diamond Pixels** craft into a whole diamond, and nine **Emerald Pixels** into an emerald.

### Ride a rhino beetle

Shrink down and you'll find **rhino beetles** trundling through the grass: big, gentle bugs as large as a horse to you. **Saddle one** and, while you're tiny, use it with an empty hand to climb on. It carries you at a good clip and **walks straight up walls**. Beetles love **sugar**: feed two and they'll make a grub that grows into a new beetle. Grow back to full size and you'll tumble off: you're far too big.

### Fly a dragonfly

**Dragonflies** zip around above the grass when you're tiny, shimmering blue-green with four glassy wings. **Saddle one** and, while you're tiny, use it with an empty hand to take off. It flies **wherever you look**, climbs while you hold jump, and hovers in place when you let go. Dragonflies love **sweet berries**: feed two and they'll make a nymph that grows into a new dragonfly.

### Leap on a grasshopper

**Grasshoppers** hop through the grass when you're tiny. **Saddle one** and, while you're tiny, use it with an empty hand to climb on. **Hold jump to wind up a leap**, just like a horse's jump bar, and let go to spring high over the grass. Falls never hurt a grasshopper or whoever's riding it. Grasshoppers love **wheat**.

### Catch fireflies

On warm, dry nights, **fireflies** drift over the grass, their tails glowing, and slip away at dawn. At full size they're little sparks; when you're tiny, they're as big as you are. Use a **glass bottle** on one to catch it in a **Firefly Jar**: a little lamp that glows all night and never needs fuel.

### Bugs are bosses now

At your size, spiders, cave spiders, silverfish, bees and endermites are giants, and they hit **twice as hard**. Beat one while you're tiny and it's a boss victory. It drops a hoard: string, spider eyes and cobwebs, with a chance of emeralds or a fresh Shrinking Draught.

### The Shrink Ray

Turn the tables. Aim the **Shrink Ray** at a mob and zap it down to bug size for 30 seconds. A shrunken mob can only do **a quarter of its usual damage** to anything full-sized, so a shrunken zombie is a nuisance and a shrunken creeper's blast barely stings. Like you, they can scuttle up walls while they're tiny. It works on almost anything except players and bosses.

### The Pixel Chisel

Love carving but don't want to shrink? Hold a **Pixel Chisel** and you can carve blocks pixel by pixel at full size, quickly, whatever the block.

## 🐉 Dragons

### The twelve dragons

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| ❄️ **Frost** | Ice: slows and freezes targets, turns water into ice you can walk on, and cools lava into obsidian | Freezes a path across water as it walks | Meat or fish |
| ⚡ **Storm** | Lightning: crackling hits that sometimes call down a lightning bolt | The fastest flier | Meat |
| 🌿 **Verdant** | Spores: poisons foes, makes plants and crops burst into growth, and turns bare dirt to grass | Slowly heals its rider | Meat, berries or apples |
| 🌊 **Tide** | Water: bowls foes over and puts them out, hurts endermen and blazes, douses fires, wets farmland, sets concrete powder, fills cauldrons and cools lava | Swims as fast as it flies, and its rider breathes underwater | Meat, fish or dried kelp |
| 💎 **Crystal** | Shards: make foes glow so you can see them through walls, and make budding amethyst grow | Crystal scales, as good as armor | Meat or amethyst shards |
| 🌑 **Shadow** | Shadow: withers and weakens foes | Its rider sees in the dark | Meat or spider eyes |
| 🪨 **Earth** | Grit: staggers foes, and when you're riding it, **tunnels through stone**, earth, sand, terracotta and ore, dropping everything it digs | Its rider mines faster, and its rocky hide is hard to knock about | Meat, or raw iron, copper or gold |
| 🍯 **Honey** | Honey: gums foes up so they can barely move, and fills beehives and bee nests to the brim | Its rider never goes hungry | Meat or honeycomb |

| 💨 **Air** | Gusts: blow foes clean away, and blow out fires | Its rider is **invisible**, and once tamed it's **invisible too** (only its owner sees a faint ghost of it). The best climber | Meat or feathers |
| 🟣 **Ender** | Ender fire: lifts targets off their feet | | Meat or chorus fruit |

Fire breath never sets your blocks on fire, storm lightning won't burn your house down, and an Earth dragon only digs when you're riding it and aiming its breath.

### Where dragons live

Wild dragons live **only on sky islands**. They guard their roosts, or carry pillagers at sky outposts. They never just appear around the world, so meeting one is always an event. Wild adults defend their territory and will come for you if you get close.

### Getting an egg

- **Defeat a wild dragon** and it drops its egg.
- **Raise one of your own:** a tamed, fully grown dragon lays an egg every two or three in-game days.

### Hatching and raising

Set an egg down and wait. It cracks a little more over a few minutes, then hatches into a hatchling that **bonds with whoever is closest**.

Hatchlings start small and grow to full size over two in-game days. They get bigger, stronger and louder as they grow, from 20 health up to 80. Feeding speeds the growing up and heals them too. Once your dragon has grown a little, it can breathe.

### Taming a wild dragon

Feed a wild dragon its favourite food and it may come round. Hatchlings and freed dragons warm to you quickly. A grown wild dragon takes patience, and it won't make it easy.

### Your dragon

- **Sit or follow:** sneak and use your dragon with an empty hand.
- **It fights for you,** attacking whatever hurts you and whatever you attack.
- **It follows you into the sky** when you glide with an elytra, fly an airship, or ride another dragon.
- **Its kills count as yours,** experience and all.
- **Breath never hurts you or your pets.**

### Riding

Put a **saddle** on your fully grown dragon (it shows on the dragon), then use it with an empty hand to climb on.

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Take off | Jump, or look up while moving forward |
| Steer | Look where you want to go |
| Climb | Hold jump |
| Gain speed | Dive by looking down |
| Land | Come down to the ground looking level or lower |
| Breathe | **V** (change it under Controls, "Dragon Breath / Airship Cannon") |
| Unleash its power | **R** (change it under Controls, "Dragon Power") |

**Bring a friend.** Once you're in the saddle, a friend can climb on behind you.

**Saddlebags.** Use a **chest** on a saddled dragon to hang saddlebags from the saddle, then press your **inventory key (E)** while riding to open 27 slots of storage. Shears take the bags off (spilling what's in them) before the saddle.

### Dragon powers

Every grown dragon has a **special power**. Press **R** while riding to unleash it, then give it half a minute to recharge. Powers only strike monsters and whatever you're fighting, never your friends or pets.

| Dragon | Power |
| --- | --- |
| 🔥 **Ember** | **Inferno:** a ring of flame sets every foe around alight |
| ❄️ **Frost** | **Blizzard:** every foe around is frozen in its tracks |
| ⚡ **Storm** | **Thunderstorm:** lightning strikes the six nearest foes |
| 🌿 **Verdant** | **Bloom:** heals you and your dragon, poisons foes, and makes the ground burst into growth |
| 🌊 **Tide** | **Tidal Wave:** throws every foe back and puts out the fires around |
| 💎 **Crystal** | **Crystal Shield:** you and your dragon are wrapped in crystal armor, and every foe nearby glows |
| 🌑 **Shadow** | **Shadow Cloak:** you and your dragon turn invisible, and foes are left blind and weak |
| 🪨 **Earth** | **Earthquake:** the ground heaves, flinging every foe around into the air and leaving them reeling |
| 🍯 **Honey** | **Swarm:** six angry bees pour out to sting your foes, then buzz off after half a minute |
| 🌈 **Rainbow** | **Rainbow Burst:** every foe is dazzled, sheep turn every colour, and you and your dragon heal and speed up |
| 💨 **Air** | **Tornado:** a whirlwind sweeps every foe together and flings them sky-high |
| 🟣 **Ender** | **Void Rift:** every foe around is hurled into the air, and falls back down hard |

### Dragon scales and dragonscale armor

A grown, tamed dragon **sheds a scale** every few minutes, and wild dragons drop a few when they're beaten. Craft **dragon scales** into **dragonscale armor**, shaped just like any other armor. It's as strong as diamond and tougher, and wearing the **full set** means **falls can't hurt you, and neither can fire**.

### Dragon armor

Craft armor from iron, gold or diamonds wrapped around leather, or upgrade diamond armor to **netherite** at a smithing table. Use it on your dragon and it **shows on the dragon**. Use another piece to swap it, or shears to take it off.

| Armor | Protection |
| --- | --- |
| Iron | 6 armor |
| Gold | 8 armor |
| Diamond | 12 armor, plus toughness |
| Netherite | 16 armor, plus toughness and knockback resistance |
| Elder | 20 armor, plus more toughness and knockback resistance |

**Elder Dragon Armor** is made from eight **Elder Scales** around netherite dragon armor. Only the Elder Dragon has those.

### The Dragon Whistle

Made from a bone, an iron nugget and string. Blow it and **every dragon of yours within earshot comes flying**. Sneak and blow it to tell them all to sit and wait.

### The Elder Dragon

High in a **Sky Citadel** lives the **Elder Dragon**, the oldest dragon in the sky. It's **more than twice the size** of any other dragon, has **200 hearts**, and shows a **boss bar** to everyone nearby. It can't be tamed or ridden, and it attacks any player who comes near.

As it weakens, its breath changes from **fire** to **frost** to **lightning** to **shadow**, and each time it roars, throwing back and hurting everyone close by. Beat it and it leaves:

- its **Dragon Heart**. Give it to your own grown dragon to make it an **Elder**: bigger, with half as much health again, harder-hitting breath, and a power that recharges faster.
- a pile of **Elder Scales**, for Elder Dragon Armor and the **Elder Blade**: two Elder Scales on a netherite sword make a blade that lets loose **fire, frost, lightning or shadow** with every blow.
- plenty of **dragon scales** and **experience**.

The Shrink Ray doesn't work on it. Bring good armor, food and friends!

### The Ender Dragon

Bring the End's **Dragon Egg** home and feed it **chorus fruit**. Every fruit is a chance that the egg finally hatches into your very own **Ender Dragon**, tamed to you. Feeding the egg also stops it from teleporting away.

## ☁️ Sky Islands

### Finding them

Floating islands drift **between y 180 and 230** above every overworld biome, about a hundred blocks apart. Look up as you explore: some islands have a spring that spills a **waterfall all the way down to the ground**, Ember islands send up **columns of smoke**, and at night glow berries give them away.

### Twelve kinds of island

Every island is different: a few ragged lobes of land with rocky spikes hanging down into the sky, packed with ore, with **diamonds toward the tips**. Each has a theme:

| Island | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| 🌼 **Meadow** | Rolling grass, oak, birch and azalea, wildflowers and fireflies, ponds, glow berries hanging beneath |
| 🌋 **Ember** | Blackstone and basalt, patches of magma, smoking vents, basalt columns, lava pools, charred trees, gilded blackstone |
| 🧊 **Frost** | Snowfields, ice spikes, frozen ponds, snow-laden spruce, icicles beneath |
| ⚡ **Storm** | Windswept rock, stone spires crowned with lightning rods that draw the storm's strikes, chains and lanterns beneath |
| 🌿 **Verdant** | Moss, flowering azalea, pink petals, spore blossoms and glow berries thick beneath |
| 🏝️ **Lagoon** | Sandy shores round a lagoon of seagrass and glowing sea pickles, palm trees, sea lantern pillars, waterfalls, prismarine and glow lichen beneath |
| 💎 **Crystal** | Calcite and giant amethyst crystals (some with budding amethyst at their heart), cherry trees in blossom, amethyst buds, crystals hanging beneath |
| 🌑 **Gloom** | Pale moss and pale oaks trailing moss, eyeblossoms that open at night, sculk, soul-lit obelisks and soul fire vents, deepslate beneath |
| 🏜️ **Canyon** | Red sand over rock striped in sunset bands of terracotta, hoodoos, outcrops of raw ore, cacti and acacias, gold in the rock, dripstone beneath |
| 🌈 **Rainbow** | A stained-glass rainbow arching over the island, prisms of coloured glass, flowering azalea, flowers, and rock in bands of every colour, full of gems |
| ☁️ **Cloud** | Lumpy white ground of wool and snow, a windmill, little clouds drifting round it, and rain streaking down from beneath |
| 🌻 **Hive** | A meadow thick with every flower, oaks and birches hung with bee nests, a giant beehive with treasure inside, honeycomb beneath and honey dripping into the sky |

There are four kinds of island:

- **Wild islands** hide a ruined shrine with a treasure chest. Expect gold, iron, emeralds, diamonds, golden apples, enchanted books, Tiny Worlds draughts, sometimes a saddle, a whistle, a chisel or a Shrink Ray, and rarely a whole airship.
- **Dragon roosts** have a nest around a **dragon's hoard**: gold above all, plus gems, golden apples, sometimes **dragon armor**, a chance of an enchanted golden apple, and **always a saddle**. A wild dragon guards it, and its kind matches the island: Ember dragons on volcanic islands, Frost dragons on snowy ones, and so on.
- **Pillager sky outposts** have a dark oak watchtower with a lookout chest, a dock with a **pillager airship**, a garrison of pillagers and a vindicator, and pillagers riding **dragons instead of ravagers**.
- **Sky Citadels** (about one island in twenty) are big Storm or Gloom islands crowned with a **ruined castle**: a crumbling wall with a gate and battlements, a lantern-topped tower at each corner, and a courtyard open to the sky, where the **Elder Dragon** guards the richest hoard in the sky. Expect diamonds, emeralds, enchanted books and gear, netherite scrap, dragon scales and armor, golden apples, and maybe even an **elytra**.

### Sky villages

About one island in twenty is a **village in the sky**: a fenced green with a well and a bell, four cottages each with a bed, a job site and a villager, a field of crops, an iron golem keeping watch, a cat, and half the time an **airship** moored on the green for travellers to borrow.

### The Storm Leviathan

In a **thunderstorm**, high in the sky, you may meet the second boss: the **Storm Leviathan**, a dark whale more than sixteen blocks long with glowing eyes, lightning scars, 300 health and a boss bar. It circles you, **charges** to ram you, and **calls lightning** down where you stand (watch for the sparks, and move!). Lightning can't hurt it. Beat it for a **trident** of Channeling and Loyalty, a **Heart of the Sea**, nautilus shells and diamonds.

### Sky whales

High over the Overworld, up where the islands float, **sky whales** swim through the air, singing. Fly up past y 110 and sooner or later one comes swimming by.

- **Ride one:** use it with an empty hand to sit on its back. Four can ride at once, and it carries you wherever it drifts.
- **Steer one:** put a **saddle** on it, and whoever sits at the front steers. It goes where you look, climbs while you hold jump, and floats when you let go.
- **Call one down:** hold a **fish** and a whale comes down to you. Feed it fish to heal it.
- **Climbing off** high in the sky is safe: the whale lets you drift down gently.
- **Calves:** some whales swim with a calf. Feed it fish to help it grow up.

### Dragon Wings

Wear **Dragon Wings** in your chest slot and glide, just like an elytra (fireworks boost them too). Craft them from six **dragon scales** and two **phantom membranes**, and mend them with dragon scales.

### Conquering an outpost

The pillagers' dragons are captives. They can't be tamed while their masters hold the outpost. Clear out **every pillager** within about 48 blocks, including the dragon riders and the airship crew, and the dragons are free. Freed dragons stop fighting, fly back to the island, and take to you quickly.

## 🎈 Airships

### Building one

```
wool    wool     wool
string  furnace  string
planks  boat     planks
```

Place it on the ground and climb aboard. It holds **up to four people**, and the first one on is the pilot.

### Flying

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Forward / back | W / S |
| Turn | A / D |
| Climb | Jump |
| Descend | Sprint |

Use fuel on the ship to fill its engine. It stores up to 30 minutes of fuel, and the time left is shown while you fly.

| Fuel | Flight time |
| --- | --- |
| Coal or charcoal | 80 seconds |
| Blaze rod | 2 minutes |
| Block of coal | 10 minutes |

Out of fuel, it can only drift slowly and sinks. Leave it empty and it hovers right where you parked it.

### Cargo hold

**Sneak and use the ship with an empty hand** to open its 27-slot cargo hold. That's a whole chest of storage that flies with you.

### Paint the sky

Use any **dye** on your airship to recolor its balloon. There are sixteen colors to choose from.

### Cannons

Craft an **Airship Cannon** (three iron ingots over gunpowder, a dispenser and gunpowder) and use it on your ship to mount it at the bow. At the helm, press **V** to fire where you're looking. Each shot burns ten seconds of fuel.

- Cannonballs hurt whatever they hit, and anyone right beside it, but **never break blocks**.
- **Four hits sink an airship.** Holes stay until you patch them with **planks**.
- They won't hit your own ship or anyone aboard her.

### Picking it up

Punch it a few times quickly to pack it back into an item. Stray arrows and crossbow bolts bounce off, so a passing skeleton can't knock you out of the sky.

### Pillager airships

The pillagers have airships too. A pillager outpost on the ground sends one up now and then when you come near, and every sky outpost keeps one docked. They fly under **dark balloons**, patrol, hunt players, and fly in close so their crews can fire crossbows at you, and **every one carries a cannon**. **Shoot the crew down and take the helm**, and the ship is yours, with a little fuel left in the tank and their plunder in the hold. Or sink it with a cannon of your own.

## ❓ FAQ

**How do I find a sky island?**
Explore new parts of your world and look up between y 180 and 230. Waterfalls pouring out of the sky are a good sign.

**How do I get up there?**
Build an airship, glide in with an elytra, or build a tower. Once you have a dragon, it can fly you. Or hold up a fish and hitch a ride on a passing sky whale.

**How do I get a dragon egg?**
Defeat a wild dragon at a roost or a sky outpost. After that, your own grown dragon lays eggs.

**My dragon won't let me ride it.**
It has to be yours, fully grown and saddled, and your hand must be empty. Feeding it helps it grow faster. (And a dragon shrunk by a Shrink Ray is far too small to carry anyone.)

**My dragon is miles away.**
Blow a Dragon Whistle and it will fly to you.

**Why can't I tame the pillagers' dragons?**
They're still captives. Defeat every pillager around the outpost first.

**How do I get back to normal size?**
Wait out the five minutes, or drink a Growth Draught.

**Does it work in my existing world?**
Yes. Sky islands only appear in parts of the world you haven't explored yet.

**Does it work in multiplayer?**
It's built for it: install it on the server and on every player's game.
