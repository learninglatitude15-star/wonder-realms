# Wonder Realms 1.2.0: Elements of the Sky

The sky islands come in five elemental themes, a new dragon hatches, dragons can wear armor, airships carry cannons, and Tiny Worlds gets two new tools.

## ☁️ Elemental sky islands

Sky islands now come in five themes, and every dragon roost is guarded by a dragon that matches its island.

| Island | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| 🌼 **Meadow** | The classic island: rolling grass, oak, birch and azalea, wildflowers, fireflies and ponds, with glow berries hanging beneath |
| 🌋 **Ember** | Blackstone and basalt with patches of magma, smoking vents you can see from far away, basalt columns, lava pools and charred trees. Gilded blackstone and gold in the rock |
| 🧊 **Frost** | Snowfields, ice spikes tipped with blue ice, frozen ponds and snow-covered spruce, with icicles hanging beneath |
| ⚡ **Storm** | Windswept rock and grass, stone spires topped with lightning rods that draw the storm's strikes, plenty of copper ore, and chains, lanterns and copper rods hanging beneath |
| 🌿 **Verdant** | Moss, flowering azalea trees, pink petals and firefly bushes, with spore blossoms and glow berries hanging thick beneath |

Shrines, nests and ruins are built from each island's own stone. From the ground, look up for the smoke columns rising from Ember islands.

## 🌿 The Verdant Dragon

A new nature dragon lives on Verdant islands. It breathes a cloud of spores that **poisons** foes, makes crops and plants **burst into growth**, and turns bare dirt into grass. It eats meat, sweet berries, glow berries or apples.

## 🛡️ Dragon armor

Armor your dragon in iron, gold, diamond or netherite, and the armor shows on your dragon. Use a piece of armor on your tamed dragon to put it on. Using another piece swaps the old one out, and shears take it off.

| Armor | Armor points | Extra |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iron | 6 | |
| Gold | 8 | |
| Diamond | 12 | +2 toughness |
| Netherite | 16 | +3 toughness and 15% knockback resistance |

To craft iron, gold or diamond dragon armor, arrange seven ingots (or diamonds) around a piece of leather:

```
ingot  .        ingot
ingot  leather  ingot
ingot  ingot    ingot
```

Upgrade diamond dragon armor to netherite at a smithing table. Dragon hoards sometimes hold armor too.

## 📯 The Dragon Whistle

Craft it from a bone, an iron nugget and string. Blow it to call every dragon of yours within 160 blocks: they stop sitting and fly to you. Sneak while blowing it to tell them all to sit.

## 👥 Room for two

Once you're in the saddle, a friend can climb on behind you.

## 💣 Airship cannons

Mount a cannon on your airship, then press **V** at the helm to fire where you're looking.

- Each shot burns ten seconds of fuel.
- Cannonballs hit hard, splash anyone standing right beside what they hit, and never break blocks.
- Four hits sink an airship. Patch the holes with **planks**.

```
iron ingot  iron ingot  iron ingot
gunpowder   dispenser   gunpowder
```

Pillager airships now fly dark balloons, and every one carries a cannon. Come within 28 blocks and they'll fire at you!

## 🎨 Dyed balloons

Use any dye on your airship to change the color of its balloon. All 16 colors work.

## 🔫 The Shrink Ray

Zap a mob from up to 24 blocks away and it shrinks to bug size for 30 seconds. A shrunken mob only does **a quarter of its usual damage** to anything full-sized, so a shrunken creeper's blast barely stings. Shrunken mobs can climb walls, just like a shrunken you. The Shrink Ray works on almost anything except players and bosses, and lasts for 64 zaps.

```
            amethyst shard
iron ingot  Shrinking Draught  iron ingot
            iron ingot
```

## 🪨 The Pixel Chisel

Craft it from an iron ingot, a stick and an amethyst shard. Hold it to carve blocks pixel by pixel without shrinking.

## 🎁 More treasure

- **Shrine chests** can hold a Pixel Chisel, a Dragon Whistle or a Shrink Ray.
- **Dragon hoards** can hold dragon armor.
- **Sky outpost chests** can hold an airship cannon.

## 🏆 12 new advancements

- **Knight of the Sky:** put armor on your dragon
- **Unbreakable:** give your dragon netherite armor
- **Here, Dragon!:** call your dragons with a Dragon Whistle
- **Green Thumb:** make plants grow with a Verdant dragon's breath
- **Room for Two:** ride a dragon with a friend
- **Dragon Collector:** tame every kind of dragon
- **Honey, I Shrunk the Mobs:** shrink a mob with a Shrink Ray
- **Tiny Terror:** shrink a creeper
- **Sculptor:** carve a pixel at full size with a Pixel Chisel
- **Paint the Sky:** dye an airship's balloon
- **Broadside!:** fire an airship's cannon
- **Shot Down:** sink a pillager airship with cannon fire

The new island themes only appear in parts of the world you haven't explored yet.
