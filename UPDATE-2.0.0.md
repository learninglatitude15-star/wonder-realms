# Wonder Realms 2.0.0: The Overcloud

A whole new world above the sky, and sixteen new dragons.

## ☁️ The Overcloud

High above the Overworld, past the clouds and through the storm, lies the **Overcloud**: a new dimension of
**floating continents over an endless sea of clouds**.

- Land at every height from just above the cloud tops to past y 280, split by deep gulfs of open sky, with
  waterfalls pouring down onto the clouds
- **Four biomes**:
  - 🌼 **Sunlit Meadows**: rolling meadows of flowers, wildflowers and pumpkins, with sheep, cows, chickens and rabbits
  - ⚡ **Storm Heights**: dark, wind-bent heights under a grey sky, crackling with sparks, where goats climb and
    Storm dragons nest
  - 💎 **Crystal Reaches**: cold, shimmering country of pink cherry groves and **amethyst geodes** in the cliffs,
    home to Crystal and Frost dragons
  - 🔥 **Dragon Peaks**: warm, dry peaks under a sunset haze where dragons of six kinds gather: Ember, Earth, Honey,
    Verdant, Air and Rainbow
- **Sky islands everywhere**: about three times as many as in the Overworld's sky, floating higher, and more often
  the special ones: dragon roosts, sky villages, pillager outposts and **Sky Citadels**, each with its Elder Dragon
- **Sky whales** swim through the Overcloud at every height, not just high up
- Coal, iron, copper, emeralds and the odd diamond in the rock

## 👁️ The Storm Eye

The way up.

- Defeat the **Storm Leviathan** and it drops one, or craft one from **four Elder Scales around a Heart of the Sea**
- Hold it, in either hand, **at y 300 or higher in the Overworld**, and the storm lifts you into the Overcloud. From
  y 200 it starts to tug upward and tells you how much higher to go
- Your **dragon, airship or sky whale comes with you**, along with everyone riding on it
- It isn't used up, so you can go back whenever you like

## 🌥️ Clouds

- The cloud sea is made of **Cloud** blocks: they're soft underfoot, and **landing on them never hurts**, from any
  height
- **Sneak to sink through.** Sink all the way out of the bottom of the cloud sea and you fall back into the
  Overworld's sky, with **Slow Falling** to drift down safely. Your mount drifts too
- Mine clouds and take them home: a pad of clouds makes a soft landing anywhere

## 🐉 Sixteen new dragons

Twenty-eight kinds of dragon now, each with its own breath, gift to its rider, power on the R key, favourite food,
egg and spawn egg.

For the first time, dragons live on the ground: each kind in its own biomes, a few at a time, appearing (like horses or
llamas) as new land is explored. They wander in peace and only fight back if you attack them, so they're there to
befriend: feed one its favourite food to tame it, or win its egg.

### And with them

- **Every dragon rears back to use its power**: up on its hind legs with its forelegs pawing the air, head thrown back
  and wings flung wide, then a beat of its wings and a roar, before it drops back down (in the air it rears up the same
  and flies on). The Elder Dragon rears up too when it roars between phases

- Two new effects: **Asleep** (a Moon dragon's: can't move, can't fight, and forgets who it was after) and **Bouncy**
  (a Slime dragon's gift: falls don't hurt)
- **Sky island roosts** can be guarded by the new dragons too, each on the kind of island most like home: the
  Overworld's islands by its eight new kinds as well as the first eleven, and the Overcloud's islands by any kind at all
- An **Echo dragon's lair** is a sculk nest on a Deep Dark cave floor
- **11 new advancements**: *Dragons of the Wild* and *Lords of the Overcloud* (tame every kind), *Boing!*, *Spring
  Fever*, *Iron Ally*, *Bubble Trouble*, *Sunbaked*, *Sweet Dreams*, *Head in the Clouds*, *Second Life* and *Now You
  See Me*. There are now 67 in all.

## 🌊 Ocean islands

A new kind of sky island: **a sea in the sky**.

- A ring of sandy beach round a little ocean, deepest in the middle
- A **coral reef** in the shallows, **kelp forests** in the deeps, seagrass and glowing sea pickles on the sand
- Tropical fish, cod and squid swimming in it, and sometimes a **Coral dragon** guarding it
- **Sunken treasure** half buried at the very bottom, by a sea lantern: gold, emeralds, diamonds, prismarine, nautilus
  shells, and often a **Heart of the Sea** (just what a Storm Eye needs), sometimes even a trident
- Palm trees on the beach and a little red and white **lighthouse**
- **It leaks!** The sea spills over the beach through channels, gushes out of cracks in the island's sides and pours
  out of holes underneath, trailing waterfalls into the sky below. (It never runs dry: its water renews itself, as an
  ocean's does.) Look for the waterfalls and you'll spot an ocean island from far away
- They float in the Overworld's sky and the Overcloud's alike
