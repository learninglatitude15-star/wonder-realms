# Wonder Realms 2.1.0: Sky Villages

Sky villages are way bigger, every one has dragon stables, and there are thirty-one new dragons: the Chameleon and
thirty more, in every corner of every world. Fifty-nine kinds in all.

## 🏘️ Sky villages

A sky village is now a whole island, about a hundred blocks across, fenced all round the edge so nobody wanders off it:

- A paved **plaza** in the middle with a well, the village bell and three market stalls
- **Four roads** out to the edge, lit by lamp posts
- **Cottages and family houses** along them, with a bed, a job site and a villager for each bed (about fourteen
  villagers in all)
- A **smithy** open to the road (blast furnace, lava, anvil, grindstone and a chest), a **library** lined with
  bookshelves, **farms** with a scarecrow, an **animal pen** and a **lookout tower**
- A plank **dock** off the end of the longest road, with the village's **airship** moored there (most villages have
  one to lend)
- Iron golems, cats, trees, flowers and waterfalls, and open meadow left between it all for the village to grow into

They float far apart in the Overworld's sky, and closer together above the Overcloud. Find one with
`/locate structure wonderrealms:sky_village`, or `/locate structure wonderrealms:overcloud_village` in the Overcloud.

## 🐉 Dragon stables

Every sky village has two dragon stables: great timber barns under steep dark roofs.

- A **stall** either side of the aisle, open to the road so its dragon can come and go, bedded with hay, with a
  trough of water
- A **hayloft** over the stalls, up a ladder
- The **tack room** at the back: a chest of riding gear and dragon food (and once in a while a dragon egg), a saddle
  and a lead hanging on the wall, and the cauldron of the leatherworker who keeps the saddles
- Most stalls have a **dragon** in them, of any kind that lives in that sky, and sometimes still young. Stabled
  dragons are gentle: they stay by their stall, never mind visitors, and take to a rider as quickly as a hatchling.
  Feed one its favourite food to make it yours
- An empty stall makes a fine home for your own dragon
- New advancement: **Stable Hand**, win over one of the dragons in a sky village's stable (68 in all)

## 🦎 The Chameleon dragon

A new dragon of the Overworld's wilds, living in savannas and birch forests (and on leafy sky islands too).

- **Camouflage**: its scales take on the colour of whatever it's standing on, grass green, sand yellow, stone grey, snow
  white, slowly changing as it goes, and the colour of the sky when it flies. Its saddle and armor blend in too
- **Keeps still, vanishes**: once it's kept still on the ground for a few seconds, monsters lose track of it
- **Breath**: a dazzling spray of shifting colours that blinds foes, makes them dizzy, and often leaves them forgetting
  who they were after
- **Gift**: monsters only notice its rider up close (within about eight blocks)
- **Power (R): Disguise!** For a minute it looks exactly like a different kind of dragon (a different one each time),
  and wild dragons of that kind take it, and its rider, for one of their own and leave you be. Attack one, though, and
  the game's up
- **Food**: spider eyes or sweet berries
- Its egg is a patchwork of every colour it might turn
- New advancement: **Master of Disguise**, and *Dragons of the Wild* now wants a Chameleon too (69 advancements)

### ⏱️ Stopping time

- The **Clockwork dragon's power** stops time: every mob, arrow in flight, falling block and the sun itself stand still,
  while you (and the dragon you ride) move freely. Use it again and time flows on. It never needs to recharge
- Clockwork dragons drop **Clockwork Scales** (a tamed one sheds them now and then, twice as often as other dragons
  shed theirs)
- Surround a **clock** with eight Clockwork Scales to make a **Time Clock**: use it to stop time, and again to start it,
  anywhere, no dragon needed
- New advancement: **Time Lord** (72 in all)

## 🔧 Fixes

- **The Storm Leviathan couldn't be hurt.** It's drawn four times a whale's size, but its hitbox stayed whale-sized,
  down at its belly, so blows aimed at it found nothing. Now its hitbox is as big as it looks
- The same went for every creature sized by the game's scale: sky whales were hard to click, and beetles, dragonflies,
  grasshoppers, fireflies and Pixel Miners had hitboxes far bigger than they are. All fixed

## ☁️ Fewer sky islands

Sky islands are half as common as before, in the Overworld's sky and over the Overcloud alike, so each one is more of
a find. (Sky villages keep their own spacing.)

Villages and the new spacing only appear in land you haven't explored yet; what you've already explored stays as it is.
