# Wonder Realms

A Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3 (needs Fabric API and GeckoLib 5.5.6 or newer) with three adventures in one:

- **Tiny Worlds**: shrink to the size of a bug, shrink your enemies too, ride a beetle up a wall, fly a dragonfly,
  and leap on a grasshopper.
- **Dragons**: win an egg, raise a dragon, armor it, fly it, unleash its power, and face the Elder Dragon.
- **Sky Islands & Airships**: explore floating islands of twelve kinds and villages in the sky, ride a sky whale,
  face the Storm Leviathan, raid pillager sky outposts, and fight battles between airships.

## What's new in 1.4

- **The Elder Dragon**: a boss more than twice the size of any other dragon, guarding the hoard of a **Sky Citadel**,
  a ruined castle in the clouds. It changes its breath from fire to frost to lightning to shadow as the fight goes on.
- **Dragon powers**: press **R** while riding to unleash your dragon's special power. Every kind has its own.
- **Dragon Hearts**: the Elder Dragon's heart turns your own dragon into an Elder: bigger, tougher and stronger.
- **Elder Dragon Armor**, the strongest dragon armor, made from the Elder Dragon's scales.
- **Dragon scales and dragonscale armor**: tamed dragons shed scales, and a full set of dragonscale armor keeps off
  falls and fire.
- **Two new dragons, with islands of their own**: the **Earth** dragon, whose breath tunnels through stone (its power
  is an **Earthquake**), lives on badlands-striped **Canyon** islands; the **Honey** dragon, whose sticky breath fills
  beehives (its power calls a **Swarm** of angry bees), lives on flower-filled **Hive** islands with a giant beehive.
- **Rainbow and Air dragons**: the **Rainbow** dragon's breath turns sheep every colour and repaints wool and glass
  (its power is a **Rainbow Burst**); the **Air** dragon turns its rider invisible, and once tamed is invisible itself,
  to everyone but its owner (its power is a **Tornado**). They live on **Rainbow** and **Cloud** islands.
- **Sky villages**: villagers living on a fenced island green, with cottages, a well, a farm, a golem, and sometimes
  an airship to borrow.
- **Dragon Wings**: glide like an elytra on wings made from dragon scales and phantom membranes.
- **The Storm Leviathan**: a second boss, a huge storm whale that hunts players in the sky during thunderstorms.
- **Sky whales**: enormous, gentle whales that swim through the sky, some with a calf. Ride on one's back with up to three friends, or
  saddle it and steer.
- **The Elder Blade**: a sword of Elder Scales that lets loose fire, frost, lightning or shadow with every blow.
- **Dragonflies** and **grasshoppers**: fly one or leap on the other when you're tiny. Hold jump to wind up a
  grasshopper's leap.
- **Fireflies**: they drift over the grass on warm nights. Catch them in a glass bottle for a **Firefly Jar** lamp.
- **22 new advancements.**
- **Fixes**: Verdant, Tide, Crystal and Shadow eggs now drop when you mine them, and wild beetles and dragonflies
  wander off when nobody's near instead of piling up.

## Tiny Worlds

**Shrinking Draught** (glass bottle + brown mushroom + amethyst shard): drink it to shrink to a tenth of your size
for five minutes. While you're tiny:

- **Climb walls** by walking into them.
- **Falls barely hurt.**
- **Carve blocks pixel by pixel.** Mining stone, dirt, wood and most other plain blocks chips away one 1/8-block
  "pixel" at a time, so you can sculpt them. The block's drop comes once you've carved half of it away.
- **Find dewdrops.** Breaking grass and ferns sometimes turns one up. Drinking one gives a moment of regeneration.
- **Bugs become bosses.** Spiders, silverfish, bees and endermites hit twice as hard. Beat one and it drops a hoard:
  string, spider eyes, cobwebs, sometimes emeralds or another Shrinking Draught.
- **Meet the Pixel Miners**, tiny folk who live in the grass and the stone. Give one a dewdrop and it pays you in
  nuggets, redstone, or now and then a Diamond or Emerald Pixel. Nine pixels craft into a whole gem.

**Growth Draught** (glass bottle + red mushroom + bone meal) brings you back to full size early.

**Shrink Ray**: aim it at a mob and use it to shrink that mob to bug size for 30 seconds. Shrunken mobs only do a
quarter of their usual damage to anything full-sized, so a shrunken creeper's blast barely stings. Like you, they
can climb walls while they're tiny. It doesn't work on players or bosses. Craft it as follows:

```
          amethyst shard
iron ingot  Shrinking Draught  iron ingot
          iron ingot
```

**Pixel Chisel** (iron ingot + stick + amethyst shard): hold it to carve blocks pixel by pixel at full size.

**Rhino beetles** trundle through the grass near you while you're tiny. Saddle one, then (while tiny) use it with an
empty hand to ride it: it's quick, and it walks straight up walls. Beetles love sugar, and two fed sugar make a grub
that grows into a new beetle. Shears take the saddle off.

**Dragonflies** zip around above the grass near you while you're tiny. Saddle one, then (while tiny) use it with an
empty hand to fly it: it goes where you look, climbs while you hold jump, and hovers when you let go. Dragonflies
love sweet berries, and two fed berries make a nymph that grows into a new dragonfly.

**Grasshoppers** hop through the grass near you while you're tiny. Saddle one, then (while tiny) use it with an
empty hand to ride it. Hold jump to wind up a leap, just like a horse's jump bar, and let go to spring high over
the grass; falls never hurt a grasshopper or its rider. Grasshoppers love wheat.

**Fireflies** drift over the ground on warm, dry nights, at any size, and slip away at dawn. Use a glass bottle on
one to catch it in a **Firefly Jar**: a little lamp (light level 13) that never needs fuel.

## Dragons

Dragons live only on **sky islands**, and they come in eleven wild kinds. Each has its own breath, and a gift it
shares with whoever rides it:

| Dragon | Breath | Gift | Food |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ember** | Fire: sets things alight, melts snow and ice, lights campfires and candles | Its rider can't burn | Meat |
| **Frost** | Ice: slows and freezes, turns water into ice you can walk on, cools lava | Freezes a path across water as it walks | Meat or fish |
| **Storm** | Lightning: crackles, and sometimes calls down a bolt | The fastest flier | Meat |
| **Verdant** | Spores: poisons foes, makes plants grow, and turns bare dirt to grass | Slowly heals its rider | Meat, berries or apples |
| **Tide** | Water: bowls foes over, puts out fires (and burning mobs), hurts endermen and blazes, wets farmland, sets concrete powder, fills cauldrons, cools lava | Swims as fast as it flies; its rider breathes underwater | Meat, fish or dried kelp |
| **Crystal** | Shards: make foes glow so you can see them through walls, and make budding amethyst grow | Crystal scales, as good as armor | Meat or amethyst shards |
| **Shadow** | Shadow: withers and weakens | Its rider sees in the dark | Meat or spider eyes |
| **Earth** | Grit: staggers foes, and when you ride it, tunnels through stone, earth, sand, terracotta and ore, dropping what it digs | Its rider mines faster (Haste II); rocky hide, hard to knock about | Meat or raw iron, copper or gold |
| **Honey** | Honey: gums foes up so they can barely move, and fills beehives and bee nests to the brim | Its rider never goes hungry | Meat or honeycomb |
| **Rainbow** | A rainbow: dazzles foes, turns sheep a new colour without hurting them, and when you ride it, repaints wool, concrete, terracotta, glass and carpet | Villagers give its rider their best prices | Meat or flowers |
| **Air** | Gusts: blow foes clean away, and blow out fires, campfires and candles | Its rider is invisible; tamed, it's invisible too (its owner sees a faint ghost); the best climber | Meat or feathers |

An Earth dragon only digs when you're riding it and aiming its breath, so it never wrecks builds on its own.

Fire breath never sets your blocks alight, and storm lightning never starts fires.

There's a twelfth, secret kind: feed the End's **Dragon Egg** chorus fruit and it may hatch an **Ender Dragon**,
whose breath lifts targets off their feet.

**Getting an egg.** Wild dragons guard roosts on sky islands. Defeat one and it drops its egg. A tamed, grown
dragon also lays an egg every two or three in-game days.

**Hatching.** Place the egg and wait. It cracks over a few minutes, then hatches a dragon that bonds with the
nearest player.

**Taming a wild dragon.** Feed it its favourite food. Wild adults take patience and will fight back. Hatchlings and
freed dragons warm to you quickly.

**Raising.** Hatchlings grow to full size over two in-game days, and feeding them speeds that up. They get bigger
and tougher as they grow, from 20 to 80 health. What your dragon kills counts as your kill, experience and all.

**Commands.** Sneak and use an empty hand on your dragon to make it sit or follow. Tamed dragons defend you, and
follow you into the air when you're gliding, on an airship, or on another dragon.

**Dragon Whistle** (bone + iron nugget + string): use it to call every dragon of yours within earshot. They stop
sitting and fly to you. Sneak and use it to tell them all to sit.

**Dragon armor**: use it on your dragon to armor it. Using another piece swaps the old one out, and shears take
armor off (and after that, the saddle). The armor shows on your dragon.

| Armor | Recipe | Armor points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iron | iron ingots around leather (see below) | 6 |
| Gold | gold ingots around leather | 8 |
| Diamond | diamonds around leather | 12, plus toughness |
| Netherite | upgrade diamond dragon armor at a smithing table | 16, plus toughness and knockback resistance |
| Elder | eight Elder Scales around netherite dragon armor | 20, plus more toughness and knockback resistance |

```
ingot  .       ingot
ingot  leather ingot
ingot  ingot   ingot
```

**Riding.** Put a saddle on a grown dragon, then use it with an empty hand to climb on. Once you're in the saddle, a
friend can climb on behind you.

**Saddlebags.** Use a chest on a saddled dragon to hang saddlebags from the saddle. While riding, press your inventory
key (E) to open them: 27 slots. Shears take the armor off first, then the saddlebags (and what's in them), then the
saddle.

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Take off | Jump, or look up while moving forward |
| Steer | Look where you want to go |
| Climb | Hold jump |
| Dive for speed | Look down |
| Land | Come down to the ground looking level or lower |
| Breathe | **V** (rebindable under Controls, "Dragon Breath / Airship Cannon") |
| Use its power | **R** (rebindable under Controls, "Dragon Power") |

**Dragon powers.** Every grown dragon has a special power. Press **R** while riding to unleash it, then wait half a
minute for it to recharge. Powers only strike monsters and whatever you're fighting.

| Dragon | Power |
| --- | --- |
| **Ember** | **Inferno**: a ring of flame sets every foe around alight |
| **Frost** | **Blizzard**: every foe around is frozen in its tracks |
| **Storm** | **Thunderstorm**: lightning strikes the six nearest foes |
| **Verdant** | **Bloom**: heals you and your dragon, poisons foes, and makes the ground burst into growth |
| **Tide** | **Tidal Wave**: throws every foe back and puts out the fires around |
| **Crystal** | **Crystal Shield**: you and your dragon are wrapped in crystal armor, and every foe nearby glows |
| **Shadow** | **Shadow Cloak**: you and your dragon turn invisible, and foes are left blind and weak |
| **Earth** | **Earthquake**: the ground heaves, flinging every foe around into the air and leaving them reeling |
| **Honey** | **Swarm**: six angry bees pour out to sting your foes, then buzz off after half a minute |
| **Rainbow** | **Rainbow Burst**: every foe is dazzled, sheep turn every colour, and you and your dragon heal and speed up |
| **Air** | **Tornado**: a whirlwind sweeps every foe together and flings them sky-high |
| **Ender** | **Void Rift**: every foe around is hurled into the air |

**Dragon scales.** A grown, tamed dragon sheds a scale every so often, and wild dragons drop a few when they're
beaten. Craft them into **dragonscale armor**, shaped like any other armor. It's as strong as diamond and tougher, and
a full set means falls can't hurt you and neither can fire.

**The Elder Dragon.** Deep in a **Sky Citadel** (see below) lives the Elder Dragon: more than twice the size of any
other dragon, with 200 hearts and a boss bar. It can't be tamed. It breathes fire, then frost, then lightning, then
shadow as it weakens, roaring and throwing back everyone near it each time it changes. Beat it for its **Dragon
Heart** and a pile of **Elder Scales**.

- **Dragon Heart**: give it to your own grown dragon to make it an **Elder**. Elders are bigger, have half as much
  health again, breathe harder, and their power recharges faster.
- **Elder Scales**: eight around netherite dragon armor make **Elder Dragon Armor**; two on a netherite sword make
  the **Elder Blade**, which lets loose fire, frost, lightning or shadow with every blow.

## Sky Islands

Floating islands drift between y 180 and 230 in every overworld biome, roughly a hundred blocks apart. Each has a
rocky underside full of ore that's richest toward the tip, and small rocks floating nearby. They come in twelve
themes:

- **Meadow**: rolling grass, oak, birch and azalea, flowers and fireflies, ponds, and glow berries hanging beneath.
- **Ember**: blackstone and basalt with patches of magma, smoking vents, basalt columns, lava pools, charred trees,
  and gilded blackstone in the rock.
- **Frost**: snowfields, ice spikes, frozen ponds, snow-laden spruce, and icicles beneath.
- **Storm**: windswept rock and grass, and stone spires crowned with lightning rods that draw the storm's strikes.
  Chains, lanterns and copper rods hang beneath.
- **Verdant**: moss, flowering azalea, pink petals, and spore blossoms and glow berries thick beneath.
- **Lagoon**: sandy shores round a lagoon of seagrass and glowing sea pickles, palm trees, sea lantern pillars, and
  waterfalls. Prismarine, sea lanterns and glow lichen beneath.
- **Crystal**: calcite and giant amethyst crystals (some with budding amethyst at their heart, so they grow back),
  cherry trees in blossom, and crystals hanging beneath. The most diamonds of any island.
- **Gloom**: pale moss and pale oaks trailing moss, eyeblossoms that open at night, sculk, soul-lit obelisks and soul
  fire vents, over deepslate.
- **Canyon**: red sand over rock striped in sunset bands of terracotta, like the badlands, with hoodoos, outcrops of
  raw ore, cacti and acacias, gold in the rock, and dripstone stalactites beneath. Earth dragons nest here.
- **Hive**: a meadow thick with every kind of flower, oaks and birches hung with bee nests, a giant beehive with a
  treasure chest inside, and honeycomb beneath, where honey drips from overflowing nests. Honey dragons nest here.
- **Rainbow**: a stained-glass rainbow arching over the island, prisms of coloured glass, birches crowned with
  flowering azalea, flowers, and rock in bands of every colour full of gems. Rainbow dragons nest here.
- **Cloud**: lumpy white ground of wool and snow, a windmill, little clouds drifting round it, and rain streaking
  down from beneath. Air dragons nest here.

**Sky villages** (about one island in twenty): a fenced green with a well and a bell, four cottages each with a bed, a
job site and a villager, a field of crops, an iron golem and a cat, and half the time an airship moored for
travellers to borrow.

**The Storm Leviathan**: in a thunderstorm, players up past y 110 may meet this second boss, a dark whale more than
sixteen blocks long with 300 health and a boss bar. It circles, charges to ram, and calls lightning down where you
stand (sparks crackle first, so move!). Lightning can't hurt it. It drops a trident of Channeling and Loyalty, a Heart
of the Sea, nautilus shells, diamonds and prismarine.

**Sky whales** swim high over the Overworld, up where the islands float. Fly up past y 110 and now and then one
comes swimming by. Use one with an empty hand to sit on its back (four can ride at once) and go wherever it drifts.
Saddle it and whoever sits at the front steers: it goes where they look, climbs while they hold jump, and floats when
they let go. Hold a fish and a whale comes down to you; feed it fish to heal it. Climb off high up and it lets you
drift down gently. Two whales in five have a **calf** beside them: too young to ride, but every fish you feed it helps
it grow up.

**Dragon Wings** glide like an elytra (fireworks boost them too). Craft them from six dragon scales and two phantom
membranes, and mend them with dragon scales.

**How to find one.** Look up! Some islands have a spring that pours a waterfall all the way to the ground, and Ember
islands send up tall columns of smoke (Gloom islands, pale ones). Fireflies and glow berries give them away at night.

There are four kinds of island:

- **Wild islands** hide a ruined shrine with a treasure chest: gold, gems, enchanted books, Tiny Worlds draughts,
  sometimes a saddle, a whistle, a chisel, a Shrink Ray, or even an airship.
- **Dragon roosts** have a nest made from what's on the island, a dragon's hoard (always with a saddle, sometimes
  dragon armor), and the wild dragon that guards it. Its kind matches the island's theme.
- **Pillager sky outposts** have a dark oak watchtower with a lookout chest, a dock with a pillager airship, a
  garrison, and pillagers riding dragons instead of ravagers.
- **Sky Citadels**, about one island in twenty, are big Storm or Gloom islands crowned with a ruined castle: a
  crumbling wall with a gate and battlements, a lantern-topped tower at each corner, and a courtyard where the
  **Elder Dragon** guards the richest hoard in the sky (look for enchanted gear, netherite scrap, and maybe an
  elytra).

**Conquering an outpost.** Once every pillager within about 48 blocks is gone (including the dragon riders and the
airship crew), their dragons are free. Freed dragons stop fighting, fly back to the island, and are easy to tame.

## Airships

Craft an airship as follows:

```
wool    wool     wool
string  furnace  string
planks  boat     planks
```

To use one:

- **Place it** on the ground and climb aboard.
- **Fuel it** with coal or charcoal (80 s), blaze rods (2 min) or coal blocks (10 min) by using them on the ship.
- **Fly it** with WASD to steer, jump to climb, and sprint to descend.
- **Out of fuel**, it drifts slowly and sinks. Left empty, it hovers where you parked it.
- **Seats**: up to four players (the first aboard pilots).
- **Cargo hold**: sneak and use the ship with an empty hand to open 27 slots of storage.
- **Balloon color**: use any dye on the ship.
- **Picking it up**: punch it a few times quickly. Stray arrows and crossbow bolts bounce off.

**Airship Cannon**: use one on your ship to mount it at the bow, then press **V** at the helm to fire where you're
looking. Each shot burns ten seconds of fuel. Cannonballs hurt whatever they hit (and anyone right beside it) but
never break blocks. Four hits sink a ship, and planks patch the holes. Craft the cannon as follows:

```
iron ingot  iron ingot  iron ingot
gunpowder   dispenser   gunpowder
```

Pillagers fly airships too, under dark balloons, and every one carries a cannon. Ground outposts launch one now and
then when a player is near, and sky outposts keep one docked. Shoot the crew off and the ship, and the plunder in
its hold, are yours to take. Or sink it with your own cannon.

## Advancements

The **Wonder Realms** tab tracks these:

- **Tiny Worlds**: shrinking, dewdrops, trading, carving and gem-crafting with pixels, defeating a bug while tiny,
  shrinking mobs (and a creeper), sculpting at full size with a chisel, riding a beetle, flying a dragonfly, and
  leaping on a grasshopper.
- **Dragons**: winning an egg, hatching, taming, riding, flying above y 256, freeing an outpost's dragon, hatching
  the Ender Dragon, armoring your dragon (and in netherite), calling it with a whistle, making plants grow with
  Verdant breath, riding with a friend, taming every kind of dragon, giving your dragon saddlebags, diving with a
  Tide dragon, lighting up a mob with Crystal breath, using your dragon's power, collecting dragon scales and
  wearing dragonscale armor, waking and defeating the Elder Dragon, giving your dragon its heart, and armoring it in
  Elder Dragon Armor, forging the Elder Blade, and tunnelling with an Earth dragon and filling a beehive with a Honey
  dragon.
- **Sky**: boarding an airship, capturing a pillager airship, dyeing a balloon, firing a cannon, sinking a pillager
  ship, riding and steering a sky whale, and catching fireflies in a jar.

## For developers

To build from this folder:

```
./gradlew build
```

The jar lands in `build/libs/`.

The build compiles against GeckoLib 5.5.7 for 26.3, looking for the jar at
`../mods/geckolib-fabric-26.3-5.5.7.jar`. If yours is somewhere else, point the build at it:

```
./gradlew build -Pgeckolib_jar=/path/to/geckolib-fabric-26.3-5.5.7.jar
```

Models, animations, textures, item models, recipes, loot tables, advancements and text are all generated by scripts
in `tools/`. Run them from this folder in this order:

```
python3 tools/generate_assets.py
python3 tools/generate_miner_assets.py
python3 tools/generate_airship_assets.py
python3 tools/generate_dragon_assets.py
python3 tools/generate_beetle_assets.py
python3 tools/generate_dragonfly_assets.py
python3 tools/generate_creature_assets.py
python3 tools/generate_gear_assets.py
python3 tools/generate_data.py
```
