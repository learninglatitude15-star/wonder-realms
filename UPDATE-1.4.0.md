# Wonder Realms 1.4.0: Rise of the Elder Dragon

The biggest update yet:

- **two bosses**: a dragon in a castle in the clouds, and a whale made of storm
- a special power for every dragon
- **four new dragons**, each with its own kind of sky island, including an Air dragon that turns invisible
- **villages in the sky**
- giant sky whales (and their calves) you can ride
- **Dragon Wings** to glide on, and a sword made from the Elder Dragon's scales
- grasshoppers to leap on and fireflies to catch
- two new kinds of armor
- 22 new advancements

## 🏰 Sky Citadels

About one sky island in twenty is now a **Sky Citadel**: a big Storm or Gloom island crowned with a **ruined castle**.

- A crumbling stone wall with a gate and battlements
- A tower at each corner with a lantern burning on top
- A courtyard open to the sky, with a dais of polished blackstone and gold
- Two treasure chests holding **the richest hoard in the sky**: diamonds, emeralds, enchanted books and gear, netherite scrap, dragon scales, dragon armor, golden apples, a chance of an enchanted golden apple, and maybe even an **elytra**

And guarding it all...

## 🐲 The Elder Dragon

The oldest dragon in the sky, and the first Wonder Realms boss.

- **More than twice the size** of any other dragon, with **200 hearts** and a **boss bar**
- It can't be tamed or ridden, and it attacks any player who comes near
- As it weakens, its breath changes from 🔥 **fire** to ❄️ **frost** to ⚡ **lightning** to 🌑 **shadow**. Each time, it **roars**, hurting and throwing back everyone close by
- It's immune to fire, and the Shrink Ray doesn't work on it

Beat it and it leaves behind its **Dragon Heart**, a pile of **Elder Scales**, plenty of **dragon scales**, and a lot of experience.

## ❤️ Dragon Hearts

Give the Elder Dragon's heart to your own grown dragon and it becomes an **Elder**:

- **Bigger**
- **Half as much health again** (120 instead of 80)
- **Harder-hitting breath**
- A **power that recharges faster**

## ⚔️ The Elder Blade

A sword forged from the Elder Dragon's scales, stronger than netherite. **Every blow lets loose one of the Elder's four breaths** at random:

- 🔥 **Fire** sets your foe alight
- ❄️ **Frost** slows and chills it
- ⚡ **Lightning** strikes it again
- 🌑 **Shadow** withers it

Make it from two **Elder Scales** on a **netherite sword**, and repair it with Elder Scales.

```
Elder Scale
Elder Scale
Netherite Sword
```

## 🛡️ Elder Dragon Armor

The strongest dragon armor: **20 armor points**, with more toughness and knockback resistance than netherite. Craft it from eight **Elder Scales** around netherite dragon armor. It shows on your dragon in gleaming gold, ivory and red.

```
Elder Scale  Elder Scale              Elder Scale
Elder Scale  Netherite Dragon Armor   Elder Scale
Elder Scale  Elder Scale              Elder Scale
```

## 🪨 New dragon: the Earth Dragon

A sandy-brown dragon flecked with stone, with grey stone horns.

- **Breath:** a blast of grit that staggers foes. While you're riding it, its breath **tunnels through stone**, earth, sand, gravel, terracotta and ore, dropping everything it digs. You can bore a tunnel straight through a mountain! It only digs when you're riding it and aiming, so it never wrecks your builds by itself
- **Gift:** its rider **mines faster** (Haste II), and its rocky hide gives it extra armor and makes it hard to knock about
- **Favourite food:** raw iron, raw copper or raw gold (or meat)
- **Power: Earthquake!** The ground heaves, flinging every foe around into the air and leaving them reeling

## 🍯 New dragon: the Honey Dragon

A golden dragon banded black like a bee, with pale wings and a striped egg.

- **Breath:** sticky honey that gums foes up so they can barely move. Breathe on a **beehive or bee nest** and it fills to the brim with honey, ready to harvest
- **Gift:** its rider **never goes hungry**
- **Favourite food:** honeycomb (or meat)
- **Power: Swarm!** Six angry bees pour out to sting your foes, then buzz off after half a minute

## 🌈 New dragon: the Rainbow Dragon

Every colour at once: its scales run red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo and violet, with a white belly and pearly wings. Its egg is a rainbow too.

- **Breath:** a real rainbow that dazzles foes. Breathe on **sheep** and they don't get hurt, they just come out a new colour! While you're riding it and aiming, it **repaints wool, concrete, terracotta, glass and carpet** in random colours
- **Gift:** villagers love it. Its rider gets the **villagers' best prices** (Hero of the Village)
- **Favourite food:** any flower (or meat)
- **Power: Rainbow Burst!** Rings of colour dazzle every foe around, sheep nearby turn every colour, and you and your dragon heal and speed up

## 💨 New dragon: the Air Dragon

A dragon as white as a cloud, with sky-blue wings.

- **It turns you invisible:** while you ride it, **you're invisible**
- **It's invisible too, once it's tamed:** nobody else can see it. Only its owner can see it, as a faint, see-through ghost of a dragon, with a shimmer in the air where it waits. Ride an Air dragon and you're a secret in the sky
- **Breath:** gusts of wind that **blow foes clean away** and blow out fires, campfires and candles
- **The best climber:** it climbs faster than any other dragon
- **Favourite food:** feathers (or meat)
- **Power: Tornado!** A whirlwind sweeps every foe around together and flings them sky-high

## 🏝️ Four new kinds of sky island

Every new dragon nests on its own kind of island.

**Canyon islands** look like floating badlands:

- Red sand over rock striped in **sunset bands of terracotta**
- **Hoodoos**: tall striped rock towers with sandstone caps
- **Outcrops of raw ore** bursting out of the ground, with a lump of solid raw iron, copper or gold at the heart
- Cacti (some in flower), dead bushes, dry grass and acacia trees
- Gold in the rock, and **dripstone stalactites** hanging beneath

**Hive islands** are a bee's paradise:

- A meadow thick with **every kind of flower**, plus sunflowers, lilacs, rose bushes and peonies
- Oaks and birches hung with **bee nests**
- A **giant beehive**: a dome of honeycomb and straw with bee nests set into its sides, a floor of honey, and a **treasure chest** inside
- Honeycomb and honey hanging beneath, with bee nests so full that **honey drips from them into the sky**

**Rainbow islands** are as bright as can be:

- A **rainbow** of stained glass arching right over the island (solid enough to walk on!)
- **Prisms**: quartz pillars topped with glass in every colour and a light
- Birches crowned with flowering azalea, and flowers everywhere
- Rock striped in **bands of every colour**, full of gems: lapis, emeralds, redstone, gold, amethyst and diamonds
- Drops of coloured glass hanging beneath

**Cloud islands** are made of cloud:

- Soft, lumpy ground of white wool and snow
- A **windmill** with four sails
- Little clouds drifting over the island and around it
- **Rain** streaking down from underneath
- White calcite and diorite rock beneath

## 🏘️ Sky Villages

About one sky island in twenty is now a **village in the sky**!

- A **fenced green** with gates, so nobody wanders off the edge
- A **well** with a little roof, and a **bell** for meetings
- **Four cottages**, each with a bed, a job site and a **villager** who lives there: librarians, farmers, armorers, cartographers and more. One cottage keeps a treasure chest
- A **field of crops** round a pool of water
- Lamp posts, an **iron golem** keeping watch, and a cat
- Half the villages have an **airship** moored on the green, with a little fuel in it, for travellers to borrow

## 🪽 Dragon Wings

**Glide like a dragon.** Dragon Wings work just like an elytra: wear them in your chest slot, jump off something high, and glide. Fireworks boost you, just like an elytra.

- Teal dragon-membrane wings, with a scalloped edge between the finger bones
- Mend them with **dragon scales**
- Craft them from six **dragon scales** and two **phantom membranes**. No trip to the End needed!

```
Dragon Scale  Phantom Membrane  Dragon Scale
Dragon Scale  Phantom Membrane  Dragon Scale
Dragon Scale                    Dragon Scale
```

## ⚡ Dragon powers

Every grown dragon now has a **special power**. Press **R** while riding to unleash it, then give it half a minute to recharge (an Elder needs only twenty seconds). Powers only strike monsters and whatever you're fighting, never your friends or pets.

| Dragon | Power |
| --- | --- |
| 🔥 Ember | **Inferno:** a ring of flame sets every foe around alight |
| ❄️ Frost | **Blizzard:** every foe around is frozen in its tracks |
| ⚡ Storm | **Thunderstorm:** lightning strikes the six nearest foes |
| 🌿 Verdant | **Bloom:** heals you and your dragon, poisons foes, and makes the ground burst into growth |
| 🌊 Tide | **Tidal Wave:** throws every foe back and puts out the fires around |
| 💎 Crystal | **Crystal Shield:** you and your dragon are wrapped in crystal armor, and every foe nearby glows |
| 🌑 Shadow | **Shadow Cloak:** you and your dragon turn invisible, and foes are left blind and weak |
| 🪨 Earth | **Earthquake:** every foe around is flung into the air and left reeling |
| 🍯 Honey | **Swarm:** six angry bees pour out to sting your foes |
| 🌈 Rainbow | **Rainbow Burst:** every foe is dazzled, sheep turn every colour, and you heal and speed up |
| 💨 Air | **Tornado:** a whirlwind sweeps every foe together and flings them sky-high |
| 🟣 Ender | **Void Rift:** every foe around is hurled into the air, and falls back down hard |

You can change the key under Controls ("Dragon Power").

## 🐉 Dragon scales and dragonscale armor

A grown, tamed dragon now **sheds a scale** every few minutes, and wild dragons drop a few when they're beaten. Craft **dragon scales** into **dragonscale armor**, using the same shapes as any other armor.

- As strong as diamond, and tougher
- Wear the **full set** and **falls can't hurt you, and neither can fire**
- Repair it with dragon scales

## 🐋 New creature: the Sky Whale

High over the Overworld, up where the sky islands float, **enormous whales swim through the air**, singing. They're more than ten blocks long, deep blue with cloud-white patches.

- **Fly up past y 110** on a dragon, an airship or elytra, and now and then one comes swimming by
- **Ride one:** use it with an empty hand to sit on its back. **Four can ride at once**, and it carries you wherever it drifts
- **Steer one:** put a **saddle** on it and whoever sits at the front steers. It goes where you look, climbs while you hold jump, and floats when you let go
- **Call one down:** hold a **fish** and a whale comes down to you. Feed it fish to heal it
- **Climb off** high in the sky and the whale lets you drift down gently, so you won't fall
- Shears take the saddle off, and a saddled whale never swims away
- **Whale calves:** two whales in five have a **calf** swimming beside them. Calves are too young to ride, but they love fish: every fish you feed one helps it grow up. Left alone, a calf grows up after a day

## 🌩️ The Storm Leviathan

The second Wonder Realms boss. When **thunder rolls** and you're up in the sky, a vast dark whale may come swimming out of the storm: the **Storm Leviathan**, longer than sixteen blocks, with glowing eyes and lightning scars down its back.

- **300 health** and a **boss bar**
- It **circles** you, then **charges** to ram you
- It **calls lightning** down where you stand. Watch for the crackle of sparks at your feet, and move!
- Lightning can't hurt it
- When the storm passes and there's no one to hunt, it swims away into the clouds

Beat it for a **trident** of Channeling and Loyalty, a **Heart of the Sea**, nautilus shells, diamonds and prismarine.

## 🪰 New creature: the Dragonfly

Shrink down and you'll find **dragonflies** zipping around above the grass, shimmering blue-green with four glassy wings.

- **Saddle one,** then, while you're tiny, use it with an empty hand to take off
- It flies **wherever you look**, climbs while you hold jump, and **hovers in place** when you let go
- Grow back to full size and you'll fall off. Full-sized players are far too big to fly one!
- Dragonflies love **sweet berries**. Feed two and they'll make a nymph that grows into a new dragonfly
- Shears take the saddle off, and a saddled dragonfly never despawns

## 🦗 New creature: the Grasshopper

Grasshoppers hop through the grass when you're tiny: long and green, with big folded back legs.

- **Saddle one,** then, while you're tiny, use it with an empty hand to climb on
- **Hold jump to wind up a leap**, just like a horse's jump bar, then let go to **spring high over the grass**. The longer you hold it, the bigger the leap
- Falls never hurt a grasshopper or whoever's riding it
- Grasshoppers love **wheat**. Feed two and they'll make a nymph that grows into a new grasshopper

## ✨ New creature: the Firefly

On warm, dry nights, **fireflies** drift over the grass with glowing tails, and slip away at dawn. At full size they're little sparks; shrink down and they're as big as you are!

- Use a **glass bottle** on a firefly to catch it in a **Firefly Jar**
- A Firefly Jar is a little lamp that **glows all night and never needs fuel**, and now and then a firefly slips out of the lid

## 🔧 Fixes

- Verdant, Tide, Crystal and Shadow eggs now drop when you mine them. Before, they just vanished!
- Wild rhino beetles and dragonflies now wander off when nobody's near, so they don't pile up wherever you've been tiny. Saddled and bred ones always stay.

## 🧩 Also new

- Spawn eggs for the Earth, Honey, Rainbow and Air dragons, the Elder Dragon, the Storm Leviathan, the sky whale, the dragonfly, the grasshopper and the firefly
- Dragon scales, Elder Scales and the Dragon Heart are in the Ingredients tab, dragonscale armor, Elder Dragon Armor and the Elder Blade in the Combat tab, and the Firefly Jar in the Functional Blocks tab

Sky Citadels, sky villages and the new kinds of island only appear in parts of the world you haven't explored yet.
