# Wonder Realms 1.4.0+: Wings and Scales

A new look for the whole mod, and much bigger wings for dragons.

## 🪽 Longer wings

- Every dragon's wings are **twice as long**: a grown dragon's wingspan goes from 3.4 to **5.9 blocks**, and the Elder Dragon boss's to almost **13**
- A **wrist joint**: the tips trail and curl as the wings beat
- Pointed, scalloped, bat-like wings, with a claw at the wrist and finger bones fanning out from it
- On the ground they fold up into a tall upside-down V, clear of both riders and the saddlebags

A dragon's hitbox is the same size as before, so spread wings can poke through blocks in tight spaces.

## 🎨 New textures

- **Dragons:** overlapping shaded scales, belly plates, eyes with pupils, teeth, nostrils and claws, and markings for each kind: glowing magma, rime, lightning, moss and flowers, waves, crystal facets, runes, rock plates, bee stripes and honeycomb, clouds, stars and gilded scales
- **Dragon armor, saddles and saddlebags:** riveted overlapping plates, a red saddle blanket trimmed with gold, and buckled flaps
- **Sky whales:** a whale-shaped tail, soft cloud patches, throat grooves and barnacles
- **Storm Leviathan:** unbroken glowing lightning scars
- **Little creatures:** lace wings for dragonflies, a glossy rhino beetle, herringbone-legged grasshoppers and better fireflies
- **Airships:** shaded balloons with rope nets, and planked hulls with nails
- **Eggs and items:** new dragon egg shells and cracks, rounder spawn eggs, and redrawn items (all but the Elder Blade)
- **And the rest:** worn dragonscale armor, the worn Dragon Wings, the Firefly Jar, the Shrunken icon and the advancements backdrop

## 🔧 Fixes

- A flying dragon's tail no longer jumps at every wingbeat
- Pixel Miners have faces: their eyes used to be painted under their helmets
- Dragons are still drawn when only their wings are on screen
