# Wonder Realms 1.3.0: Sea, Crystal and Shadow

Three new dragons, three new kinds of sky island, a gift from every dragon, saddlebags, and a new creature to ride in the tiny world.

## 🐉 Three new dragons

### 🌊 Tide Dragon

- **Breath:** a jet of water that **knocks foes over** and puts out anything that's burning. Endermen, blazes and other mobs that hate water take extra damage.
- **Around the world:** its breath puts out fires, campfires and candles, waters farmland, turns concrete powder into concrete, fills empty cauldrons, and cools lava into obsidian.
- **Gift:** it **swims as fast as it flies**, and you can **breathe underwater** while riding it. Ride it into the sea and keep going: just look where you want to swim. Other dragons are slow underwater.
- **Eats:** meat, fish or dried kelp.
- **Lives on:** Lagoon islands.

### 💎 Crystal Dragon

- **Breath:** a spray of crystal shards that makes foes **glow for 10 seconds**, so you can see them through walls.
- **Around the world:** budding amethyst it breathes on rings back, and its crystals grow faster.
- **Gift:** its **crystal scales work like armor**: +6 armor points, on top of any dragon armor you give it.
- **Eats:** meat or amethyst shards.
- **Lives on:** Crystal islands. Its egg gives off a soft glow.

### 🌑 Shadow Dragon

- **Breath:** shadow that **withers** foes and **weakens** them, so they hit softer.
- **Gift:** whoever rides it **sees in the dark**.
- **Eats:** meat or spider eyes.
- **Lives on:** Gloom islands.

## ☁️ Three new kinds of sky island

| Island | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| 🏝️ **Lagoon** | Sandy shores around a lagoon full of seagrass and **glowing sea pickles**, palm trees, sugar cane, prismarine pillars topped with sea lanterns, and one or two waterfalls. Prismarine, sea lanterns and glow lichen hang beneath, and there's lapis and copper in the rock |
| 💎 **Crystal** | White calcite, **giant amethyst crystals** (half of them with budding amethyst inside, so you can farm them), cherry trees in blossom, alliums, lilacs and amethyst buds. Crystals hang beneath, and the rock holds more diamonds than any other island |
| 🌑 **Gloom** | Pale moss, pale oaks draped in hanging moss, **eyeblossoms that open at night**, sculk, deepslate pillars lit by soul lanterns, and soul fire vents sending up columns of smoke. Hanging moss and soul lanterns dangle beneath, with deepslate ores in the rock. Watch out for wither roses! |

Each new island has its own ruins, nests and treasure, and its roosts are guarded by the matching dragon. Three in ten wild islands are now meadows, and each other theme makes up one in ten.

## 🎒 Saddlebags

Use a **chest** on a saddled dragon to hang saddlebags from the saddle. While riding, press your **inventory key (E)** to open them: 27 slots of storage that fly with you. Without saddlebags, E opens your own inventory as usual.

- The saddlebags show on your dragon.
- Shears take off armor first, then the saddlebags (spilling what's inside), then the saddle.
- If your dragon dies, the saddlebags drop with everything still in them.

## 🪲 New creature: the Rhino Beetle

Shrink down with a Shrinking Draught and you'll find **rhino beetles** wandering through the grass. To you, they're as big as a horse.

- **Saddle one,** then, while you're tiny, use it with an empty hand to ride it.
- It's fast, and it **walks straight up walls**.
- Grow back to full size and you'll fall off. Full-sized players are far too big to ride one!
- Beetles love **sugar**. Feed two of them and they'll make a grub that grows into a new beetle.
- Shears take the saddle off, and a saddled beetle never despawns.

## 🏆 4 new advancements

- **Packed for the Journey:** give your dragon saddlebags
- **Deep Dive:** ride a Tide dragon underwater
- **Shine Bright:** make a mob glow with a Crystal dragon's breath
- **Beetle Rider:** ride a rhino beetle while you're tiny

**Dragon Collector** now asks you to tame all eight kinds of dragon.

## 🧩 Also new

- Tide, Crystal and Shadow eggs, plus spawn eggs for the three new dragons and the rhino beetle.
- Pillagers at sky outposts can now ride any of the seven wild kinds of dragon.

The new islands only appear in parts of the world you haven't explored yet.
